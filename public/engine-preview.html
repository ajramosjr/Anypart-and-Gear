The turbo system

Air filter→Compressor→Intercooler→Intake

Exhaust spins the turbine. A shared shaft drives the compressor, which pushes more air into the engine. The intercooler cools that compressed air.

Intake air and exhaust gas follow separate paths. The intercooler is usually mounted away from the engine; it is shown beside it here for learning.

The four-stroke cycle

Each piston repeats four strokes. The crankshaft turns twice for one complete cycle.

What happens when you accelerate?

1
More air and fuel

The engine controls admit more air and supply the appropriate amount of fuel.

2
Exhaust energy drives boost

As exhaust flow increases, the turbo can compress more intake air. A wastegate controls exhaust flow through the turbine to help regulate boost.

3
The crankshaft delivers rotation

Combustion pushes the pistons down. Connecting rods turn the crankshaft, which sends power toward the transmission.
