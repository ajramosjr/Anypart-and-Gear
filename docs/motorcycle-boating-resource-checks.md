## Motorcycle schools

School riding location is Heckscher State Park, Field 7, East Islip: https://bigapplemotorcycleschool.com/training-locations/ . Hicksville is the office/mailing address, not the MSF riding range. MSF corroboration: https://msf-usa.org/ride-day-events/big-apple-motorcycle-school-may-25/ and https://nysmsp.org/big-apple-motorcycle-school-msf-ride-day-may-26/ . The official homepage lists current October 2026 riding courses at Kingsborough Community College, College of Staten Island and Lehman College. No invented Manhattan or Queens riding site is listed.

## Boating and maritime training

- The WaterFront Center, 1 West End Avenue, Oyster Bay; phone 516-922-7245. Official lessons and sailing courses: https://www.thewaterfrontcenter.org/sailing-programs . Independently listed by US Sailing for Basic Keelboat: https://www.ussailing.org/education/adult/certification-courses-endorsements/find-a-school/ . On-water sailing is distinguished from powerboat handling and New York motorboat safety certification. The 2026 Basic Keelboat certification sessions have concluded; current private/group sailing lessons are described without promising available certification dates.
- The Nautical School of Maritime Licensing, headquarters 178 West Hoffman Avenue, Lindenhurst; phone 631-226-7300. Official course schedule: https://nauticalschool.com/schedule/ . Physical classroom sites: https://nauticalschool.com/contact/ . US Coast Guard National Maritime Center approved course records include The Nautical School of Maritime Licensing (NAUSCH): https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/courses/courses.pdf . Specific approval is course-dependent; no blanket accreditation claim is made. Captain-license classroom study is explicitly distinguished from practical on-water boat handling. Dates and the actual classroom location must be confirmed when booking.

Checks verify official web presence, named training locations and relevant DMV/US Sailing/Coast Guard records; they are not a site inspection or a guarantee of training quality, admission, certification or current seat availability. Descriptions are original summaries with direct official links; no third-party logos or photos were copied.
